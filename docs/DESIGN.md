# Design System: DompetKu

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Bento Wallet"**

DompetKu is a lunchbox of money: every figure the owner needs sits in its own compartment, and the compartments fit together into one tray. In light mode ("Bento") the tray is a cool grey ground, the compartments are white tiles with soft 24px corners and a whisper of an edge, and one ink tile holds the answer to "am I okay this month?". An electric blue marks what you can press and the one live measure on each tile. In dark mode ("Midnight") the same tray goes near-black under a faint violet glow, the tiles turn graphite, the hero tile becomes a deep violet gradient, and the accent shifts to violet.

Density is moderate and even. Every tile is a door: on the dashboard each tile is a button or a list of links, and tapping it opens a detail Panel (a bottom sheet on phones, a 440px right panel on desktop) with the rows behind the figure. One typeface, Manrope, does all the work. Amounts are painted at weight 800 with tabular figures and tight -0.04em tracking, so a figure reads as one shape. Navigation floats: a 116px icon rail on desktop and a dark tab bar with a raised accent button on phones. Neither touches the screen edge.

The token names in `app.css` are historical. `--papan*` is the hero tile and `--kuning*` is the accent. They no longer mean "board" and "yellow". Keep the names; read them by role.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cool grey ground, white 24px tiles with a 1px edge, one ink hero tile per page.
- Electric blue (light) or violet (dark) only on pressables and the one live measure of a tile.
- Manrope only. Amounts are 800, tabular, -0.04em, with a small raised "Rp".
- Floating chrome: a 116px icon rail on desktop, a floating tab bar with a raised FAB on phones.
- Every dashboard tile opens a detail Panel.

## Colors

A cool neutral tray with one saturated accent per mode and a green and red pair reserved for money.

### Primary
- **Ink Tile / papan** (`colors.papan`): the hero tile on each page (dashboard "Sisa bulan ini", summary tiles on Tabungan, Kasbon, Laporan and Sumber dana), the quick-entry amount tile, the phone tab bar, and the auth and welcome hero bands. In light mode it shares its hex with ink. In dark mode it is `#1b1828`, and the hero tile is painted as a 160deg gradient from `#1f1b30` to `#121218` with a 1px border.
- **Deep Ink / papan-deep** (`colors.papan-deep`): surfaces drawn on top of the hero band. Dark: `#13111d`.
- **Hero Ink / papan-ink** (`colors.papan-ink`): numerals and text on the hero tile. Dark: `#f3f2f8`.
- **Hero Muted / papan-soft** (`colors.papan-soft`): captions on the hero tile and inactive tab-bar icons. Dark: `#8d8da0`.

### Secondary
- **Electric Blue / kuning** (`colors.kuning`, hover `colors.kuning-hover`, ink `colors.kuning-ink`): the FAB, primary buttons, the active chip on the hero tile, the gauge arc, the top heat step, the active tab icon, the quick-entry cursor, text selection and the input caret. Dark mode: **Midnight Violet** `#8b7cff`, hover `#9d90ff`, ink `#0b0a14`.
- **Blue Wash / accent-wash** (`colors.accent-wash`): icon discs in menu cards, the transfer quick-capture disc and the "50/30/20" badge. Dark: `#8b7cff22` with `#b4aaff` text.

### Tertiary
- **Masuk Green** (`colors.masuk`): money in, always with "+". Its wash (`colors.masuk-wash`) fills in-icon discs and positive insights. Dark: `#3ddc97`, wash `#3ddc971f`.
- **Keluar Red** (`colors.keluar`): money out, always with "−". It is also the destructive and error colour. Its wash (`colors.keluar-wash`) fills out-icon discs. Dark: `#ff6b6b`, wash `#ff6b6b1f`.
- **Warn Amber** (`colors.warn`, wash `colors.warn-wash`): warning insights only. Dark: `#ffb547`, wash `#ffb5471a`.
- **Chart set**: chart-1 is the accent; then `colors.chart-orange`, `colors.chart-lilac`, `colors.chart-teal`, `colors.chart-grey`. The asset bar uses chart-1, chart-teal and chart-grey for wallet, savings and kasbon. Dark set: `#8b7cff`, `#ffb547`, `#ff6b9a`, `#3ddc97`, `#5ab8ff`.
- **Heat ramp**: `colors.heat-0` to `colors.heat-3`, then the accent as step 4. Dark: `#1a1a23`, then violet at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%.
- **User colours**: wallets, goals and categories carry a user colour. It fills wallet icon tiles, savings-ring arcs, donut slices and selected chips, with ink computed for contrast.

### Neutral
- **Cool Ground** (`colors.ground`): the page. Dark: `#09090d` with a radial violet glow (`#8b7cff24`, 900 x 520px at 72% -12%).
- **Tile White** (`colors.card`): tiles, the rail, chips and fields. Dark: `#121218`; popovers `#16161e`.
- **Sunken** (`colors.sunken`): bar and ring tracks, row icon tiles, segmented tracks, rail hover. Dark: `#1a1a23`.
- **Ink** (`colors.ink`): text, active chips, the active rail item, the active period. Dark: `#f3f2f8`. In dark mode active fills use `#22222d` (rail) or `#2a2a36` (chips, periods, segments).
- **Muted Ink** (`colors.muted-ink`): tile captions, dates, notes and inactive nav. Dark: `#8d8da0`.
- **Tile Edge** (`colors.border`) and **Field Stroke** (`colors.input-border`): tile edges, row rules and chip outlines. Field Stroke also marks field borders and the hover edge of a pressable tile. Dark: `#24242e` and `#2e2e3a`.

### Named Rules
**The Accent Means Touch Rule.** The accent marks things you can press and the single live measure on a tile (gauge arc, top heat step, active nav icon). It is never a heading tint, a background wash for a whole tile, or decoration.

**The Money Colour Rule.** Masuk and keluar mark money in and out, and nothing else. Keluar also marks errors and destructive actions. Warn is only for warning insights.

**The Signed Money Rule.** Every directional amount carries "+" or "−" alongside its colour. Colour alone never says in or out.

**The One Hero Tile Rule.** Each app page has one papan hero tile, first in reading order, holding the page's answer. All other tiles are white (graphite in dark mode).

## Typography

**Display Font:** Manrope (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Manrope (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)

**Character:** One geometric grotesk across the whole app. Weight 800 with negative tracking paints the figures and titles. Weights 400 to 600 do the reading, and the body carries -0.01em tracking.

### Hierarchy
- **Hero headline** (700, `clamp(2.75rem, 8vw, 5rem)`, 1.02, -0.045em): the welcome hero. The auth band uses `clamp(2.75rem, 4.5vw, 4.25rem)`, desktop only.
- **Display amount** (800, `clamp(2.5rem, 10.5vw, 3.75rem)`, 1, -0.04em, tabular): the hero figure on Tabungan, Kasbon, Laporan and Sumber dana. The dashboard hero uses `clamp(2.5rem, 11vw, 3.6rem)`. The welcome preview uses `clamp(2.5rem, 11vw, 3.5rem)`, and quick entry uses `clamp(2.5rem, 11vw, 3.25rem)`.
- **Tile amounts** (800, -0.04em, tabular): detail sheets 2.75rem; goal cards 2.5rem; fund-source cards 2.1rem; total assets and kasbon cards 2rem; panel totals 1.5rem; masuk and keluar tiles 1.3rem on phones and 1.5rem from 640px; panel rows 1.125rem and 1rem; recent rows 0.95rem.
- **Section display** (700, 1.875rem on phones, 2.75rem from 768px, 1): welcome section titles only.
- **Page title** (800, 1.625rem, 1.875rem from 1024px, 1.1, -0.04em): every page h1. The dashboard greeting ("Halo, Andre") is 1.375rem, rising to 1.875rem from 1024px.
- **Dialog title** (800, 1.375rem, 1.15, -0.03em): Panel and dialog titles.
- **Insight figure** (800, 1.375rem, 1.5rem from 640px, -0.04em): the figure pulled out of an insight. It is 2.25rem in its Panel.
- **Wordmark** (800, 1.3rem, 1, -0.045em): "DompetKu".
- **Section title** (700, 1.25rem, 1): section h2 on list pages. **Tile title** (800, 1.125rem, -0.03em): headings inside a tile.
- **Body** (400 to 700, 0.875rem): rows, descriptions and Panel content. Fields are 0.9375rem; the primary submit is 1rem; insight text and the insight Panel are 0.8125rem and 0.95rem.
- **Tile caption** (600, 0.8125rem): the sentence-case line at the top of a tile ("Total aset", "Kalender belanja"). Chips, period switches and quick-capture buttons share 0.8125rem.
- **Caption** (0.75rem): row dates, hints, errors and the hero usage pill. **Note** (500, 0.72rem): the line under a flow amount. **Heat day** (700, 0.7rem): day numbers in the heatmap.
- **Label** (600, 0.6875rem, 0.09em, uppercase): one caps label per figure, on list pages.
- **Micro** (600 to 700, 0.68rem): rail item labels, the heat legend and the "50/30/20" badge. This is the floor for running text.

The raised "Rp" in every amount is 0.5em, weight 700, no tracking, raised 0.55em, with a 0.15em right margin, at 55% opacity.

### Named Rules
**The One Face Rule.** Manrope is the only typeface. Make hierarchy with size, weight and tracking.

**The Painted Numeral Rule.** Every money amount renders through Money (or the `papan-num` style): 800, tabular lining figures, -0.04em, raised small "Rp". Never set an amount in proportional body text.

**The Label Once Rule.** A caps label names a figure, once. Caps labels never sit above headings as kickers. Tile captions are sentence case.

## Layout

The app is a bento grid. Phones get a 2-column tile grid with 12px gaps. From 1024px it becomes a 12-column grid with 20px gaps, beside a 116px rail column. Content sits in a centred container up to 1320px. Gutters are 14px on phones, 24px from 640px and 32px from 1024px. Sections are 24px apart on phones and 28px on desktop. Phone pages reserve 128px of bottom padding for the floating tab bar.

Dashboard tiles, desktop spans: hero 5, masuk 2, keluar 2, total assets 3, donut 5, insights 7, heatmap 4, recent 5, savings rings and wallets 3. On phones the hero spans both columns, masuk and keluar sit side by side, and a row of three quick-capture buttons (Keluar, Masuk, Pindah) follows. Every other tile spans both columns. The period switch sits in the header on desktop and as a full-width 3-up switch under the greeting on phones.

Tile padding is 20px, or 24px from 640px; flow tiles use 16px and 20px. The hero tile is at least 13rem tall, and 15.5rem on desktop.

Panels are bottom sheets on phones (max 92dvh, 24px top corners, a 40 x 6px handle) and 440px right panels from 768px. They have 20px side padding, or 24px from 640px, with a pinned footer for the primary action.

Touch targets stay at least 44px. Chips grow to 44px under coarse pointers. Inputs are forced to 16px on phones to stop iOS zoom.

## Elevation & Depth

The tray is near-flat. In light mode each tile has a 1px edge and a 1px whisper shadow. In dark mode tiles have the edge only, and the ground carries a faint violet glow. Real lift is reserved for chrome that floats over content: the FABs, the phone tab bar and dialogs. Rows inside a tile are split by 1px rules or by hover tint, never by nested shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Tile whisper** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px #0f12220a`, dark `none`): every tile.
- **Accent glow** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 24px` accent at 40%; 26px at 45% on the tab-bar FAB): the two FABs only.
- **Tab bar float** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px #0f122240`): the phone tab bar. In dark mode it is `#16161ef2` with a backdrop blur and a 1px border.
- **Segment lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px #0b0c0f1f`; `0 1px 3px #0f12221f` in the account sheet): the selected option in a segmented control.
- **Field focus halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px` ring at 22%): focused fields.
- **Dialog float** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 80px #0b0a1450`): dialogs over the `#05050a99` scrim, which sheets share.

### Named Rules
**The Floating Chrome Rule.** Only things that float over content (FABs, the tab bar, dialogs) cast a real shadow. Tiles stay at the whisper.

**The Midnight Glow Rule.** The violet glow belongs to the dark ground and the dark hero tile gradient. Ordinary tiles stay flat graphite.

## Shapes

Soft bento corners on a 16px base radius. Tiles are 24px and the hero tile is a step rounder at 26px. The rail, the tab bar and sheet tops are 24px; dialogs are 22px. Insight cards are 20px. Rail items, the full-width period switch and empty states are 16px. Fields are 14px, as are row icon tiles. Buttons, flow icon discs and the inner period cells are 12px. Wallet icon tiles are 10px, heat cells 9px, and category swatches and legend squares 4px. The FABs are rounded squares (18px on the rail, 19px in the tab bar). Chips, segmented controls, the desktop period switch, status pills, bar tracks and the avatar are full pills or circles. Charts use round caps: the gauge is a half ring, savings goals are full rings, and spending is a donut with a gap between slices.

The logo is an ink tile (14/48 radius) holding two planks: a short white one and a long accent one. In dark mode the tile inverts to `#f3f2f8`, the short plank to `#0b0a14`, and the long plank turns violet.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 12px radius, 48px tall in Panel footers, 44px in empty states.
- **Primary (kuning):** accent with accent ink, weight 600. Hover deepens to kuning-hover. A press scales to 0.97 over 160ms on the expo-out curve. Disabled drops to 45% opacity.
- **FAB:** a 56px accent rounded square with an accent glow. On desktop it sits at the foot of the rail. On phones it rises 28px out of the middle of the tab bar.
- **Destructive:** an outlined keluar icon button. The first tap asks "Yakin hapus?" and fills it keluar.
- **Quick capture (phones):** three white tiles, 56px tall, each with a 28px tinted icon disc.

### Chips
- **Style:** a 36px pill (44px on touch), white fill, 1px edge, 0.8125rem at weight 500. Hover darkens the outline.
- **State:** the active chip inverts to ink with ground-coloured text (`#2a2a36` in dark mode). On the hero tile, chips are a 6% white fill with a 12% white outline, and the active chip turns accent. Category and wallet chips lead with a 10px colour dot and fill with the user colour when selected.

### Cards / Containers (tiles)
- **Corner Style:** 24px; the hero tile 26px.
- **Background:** white tile, ink hero tile (dark: violet gradient), or sunken.
- **Shadow Strategy:** tile whisper (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px tile edge. A pressable tile's edge darkens to field stroke on hover, and the tile scales to 0.99 on press.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px, 24px from 640px.
- **Tile caption:** a muted 0.8125rem line at the top with an up-right arrow (or a count or link) on the right. It tells the reader the tile opens.
- **Empty state:** a dashed 1px edge at 16px radius, with a 48px sunken icon tile, a title, one line of text and an optional primary button.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 48px tall, 14px radius, a 1px field-stroke border, white fill, 0.9375rem text, and an accent caret.
- **Focus:** the border turns ring colour with a 3px halo at 22%.
- **Error:** the border turns keluar. The message sits below in 0.75rem keluar at weight 500, with `role="alert"`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put the page's answer on one hero tile, first in reading order, at display-amount size.
- **Do** build screens from 24px white tiles with a 1px edge on the cool ground, in a 2-column (phone) or 12-column (desktop) grid.
- **Do** make every summary tile pressable and open its rows in a Panel.
- **Do** render every amount through Money, with a sign whenever direction matters.
- **Do** keep the accent on pressables and the one live measure of a tile.
- **Do** keep 44px touch targets and the 2px ring focus outline at a 3px offset.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second typeface.
- **Don't** fill a whole tile or large surface with the accent; it is for buttons, the FAB and data marks.
- **Don't** give ordinary tiles a real shadow or a gradient; lift belongs to floating chrome, and gradients to the dark hero tile and dark ground glow.
- **Don't** use masuk or keluar outside money (keluar may mark errors and destructive actions).
- **Don't** show in or out by colour alone.
- **Don't** put a caps label above a heading or label a figure twice.
- **Don't** set running text below 0.68rem.
